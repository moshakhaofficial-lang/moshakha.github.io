# Keyword research — car cleaning & detailing

**Date:** 24 August 2026
**Method:** live SERP inspection. Competition ratings below come from *who actually
ranks* on page one, which I checked directly.

> ⚠️ **No search-volume figures appear in this document.** Volume and keyword-difficulty
> scores require a paid tool (Ahrefs, Semrush, Moz). I don't have one connected, and
> inventing plausible-looking numbers would be worse than having none — you'd plan
> against fiction. Everything below is grounded in observed SERP composition instead,
> which is the more decision-relevant signal anyway. If you want real volumes, a month
> of Semrush or Ahrefs Lite is the cheapest way to get them.

---

## The competitive picture

Page one for nearly every term in this niche is held by **retailer blogs and Shopify
stores**, not high-authority media:

- Jimbo's Detailing, Chemical Guys, The Rag Company, Beneath Zero, Microfiber Wholesale,
  Kleen-Rite, work-stuff.com, Esoteric
- Plus small affiliate sites: carwashwiz, carcaretotal, detailingenthusiast

**Two exploitable weaknesses:**

1. **Retailers structurally cannot be honest.** A shop selling 1600 GSM towels will never
   publish "1600 GSM is the wrong choice for glass and most interiors." That sentence is
   true, useful, and unavailable to our competition. Editorial independence is a real
   moat here, not a slogan.
2. **Cannibalization.** Jimbo's Detailing ranks *four near-duplicate URLs* for microfibre
   washing. They've split one topic across four thin pages. One genuinely complete page
   beats that.

**Where NOT to fight:** `best car wax` and variants. That SERP has Parkers, AOL,
AutoGuide and a stack of mature review sites — real media with real domain authority.
A brand-new site loses that fight for a year or more. (This was on the original brief
as a candidate; the data says defer it.)

---

## Strategic wedge: spec literacy

Every competitor repeats "higher GSM = better" because they sell high-GSM towels. The
accurate position — **GSM measures density, not quality, and the right GSM depends
entirely on the task** — is contrarian, verifiable, genuinely useful, and already the
voice the site claims. Build the cluster around it.

## ⚠️ Cannibalization risk with the existing 10 posts

This is the most important architectural finding. Three legacy posts overlap the new
cornerstone topics. If we publish both without a plan, we recreate exactly the mistake
that makes Jimbo's Detailing beatable.

| Legacy URL (keep) | Overlaps | Resolution |
|---|---|---|
| `/blog/microfibre-care-101` | Washing/care guide | **Make the legacy URL the canonical care guide.** Rewrite and expand it in place; do NOT create a second washing page. It already has whatever age and indexing exist. |
| `/blog/500-gsm-vs-800-gsm` | GSM pillar | Keep narrow: a specific head-to-head that links *up* to the pillar. Pillar covers the full range. |
| `/blog/glass-cleaning-mastery` | Streaks troubleshooting | Keep as glass *technique*; the streaks page handles *diagnosis*. Cross-link both ways. |

Remaining legacy posts (`1200-gsm-twisted-loop`, `1600-gsm-king`, `big-drying-towel`,
`wheel-rim-care`, `waterless-wash-guide`, `perfect-interior-detail`,
`ceramic-coating-guide`) have **no overlap** — they become supporting pages linking up
into the new pillars. That's a healthy hub-and-spoke structure.

## ⚠️ Product data integrity

The guides need real products. I will use products that genuinely exist and are
established in this market (The Rag Company, Meguiar's, Chemical Guys, Griot's and
similar), but:

- **Every ASIN, price and specification must be verified against the live Amazon listing
  before publish.** I will not invent ASINs — `asin` stays `null` until you supply
  verified values, and the CTA renders a visible "Link pending" chip in the meantime.
- **No hardcoded prices in body copy.** Amazon Associates prohibits stating prices that
  can go stale; we use ranges and link out for live pricing.
- Ratings are our editorial judgement, presented as such — never dressed up as lab
  testing we haven't done.
