The advantage of magnetic mounting over a clamp mount isn't convenience, exactly — it's
that the failure mode of clamp mounts happens at the worst possible moment.

## The actual problem with clamp mounts

A clamp mount needs two hands and a moment of visual attention to seat the phone
correctly between the arms. That's a minor inconvenience in a driveway and a genuine
distraction risk at a junction, which is exactly when people reach for their phone
mount. Magnetic mounting removes that step entirely — align and release, one hand, done.

## Where it holds up

**Attach and release are genuinely one-handed.** There's no clamp to squeeze open, no
arms to adjust for a case, and nothing to fumble while also watching traffic.

**No moving parts to wear out.** Clamp mounts lose spring tension over months of use;
a magnet doesn't degrade the same way.

**Two mounting options.** The suction base and the adhesive backing cover both the
windscreen and a smooth dashboard, which matters because suction cups don't hold on
textured plastic — more on that below.

## Where it falls short

**It only works with MagSafe, or a magnetic ring stuck to the phone or case.** This is
the tradeoff for one-handed operation: a clamp mount grips any phone regardless of case;
a magnetic mount grips only what has a magnet to grip. If your phone has neither, this
isn't a fit.

**Thick cases weaken the hold.** Magnetic strength falls off with distance, and a rugged
case adds distance. Test the hold with your actual case before trusting it on a real
drive, particularly over rough roads.

**Suction cups need glass, not textured dash plastic.** This is true of suction cups
generally, not a flaw specific to this mount — use the windscreen mounting point if your
dashboard has any grain or texture to it.

## Should you buy it?

**Yes, if** your phone has MagSafe or you're willing to fit a magnetic ring, and you want
mounting that doesn't require two hands or visual attention to use.

**No, if** you run a thick protective case you're not willing to swap, or your phone has
no path to a magnetic attachment point — a clamp mount will serve you more reliably in
either case.
