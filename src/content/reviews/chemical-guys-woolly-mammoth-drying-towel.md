The Woolly Mammoth is one of the most widely sold drying towels there is, and it earns
that on softness and price rather than on performance.

## What it does well

It is genuinely, noticeably plush. Running it over a panel feels cushioned in a way that
twisted loop towels don't, and for someone nervous about putting marks in their paint,
that reassurance has real value. A person who dries confidently with a soft towel will
get a better result than someone dabbing anxiously with a technically superior one.

At 25x36in it covers a lot of ground, and it's frequently discounted well below the
premium alternatives.

## The trade-off nobody mentions

Plush pile and twisted loop pile handle water differently. Twisted loops **draw water up
into the pile** and keep the contact surface relatively free, so the towel keeps gliding.
Plush pile holds water closer to the surface and releases it into the body of the towel
more slowly.

The practical result: at a similar size, the Woolly Mammoth reaches the point of
saturation sooner. Once saturated, it stops lifting water and starts pushing it around —
which is when streaking and water spots appear.

On a sedan you may never notice. On a large SUV you will be wringing it out well before
you finish.

## Shedding

Some first-wash lint shedding is normal and widely reported. Wash it two or three times
on its own before it touches paint. This isn't a defect; it's characteristic of deep
plush pile, and it settles.

## Drying time

This towel takes a long time to air-dry, which matters more than it sounds. A microfibre
towel put away damp develops mildew, and mildew permanently affects both smell and
absorbency. If you don't have somewhere to hang a large towel with airflow, that's a real
argument for a thinner one.

## Should you buy it?

**Yes, if** you want maximum softness, you're washing a normal-sized car, and price
matters. It's a lot of towel for the money.

**No, if** you're drying something large or you want the fastest possible drying — in
which case a twisted loop towel of the same size will finish the job with less wringing.
