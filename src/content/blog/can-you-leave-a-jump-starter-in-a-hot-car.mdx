A jump starter lives in the car for one reason: the day your car won't start, it needs to
be there. That makes it tempting to simply leave it in the boot all year.

The trouble is that **a modern jump starter is a lithium battery**, and lithium batteries
have opinions about temperature.

## What heat does

The inside of a closed car in summer sun can be dramatically hotter than the outside air.
Sustained heat:

- **Accelerates ageing**, so the unit holds less charge and delivers less current each year.
- **Can trigger protection circuits** that stop the unit working until it cools.
- **Is outside the published storage range** on many units, which is something to check
  in your own manual.

It isn't usually dramatic on a single hot afternoon. It's the **repeated summers** that
shorten a battery's life.

## What cold does

Cold is a different problem. A chilled lithium pack can't deliver current as easily, and
an engine in winter wants more of it, not less. A unit that would start a warm car happily
may struggle when everything is frozen.

## Where to keep it

- **Out of direct sun.** Under a seat or in a shaded boot compartment, not on the dash or
  rear parcel shelf.
- **Indoors in extremes.** In a heatwave or hard freeze, take it inside and carry it out
  when you head off on a long trip.
- **Charged, not empty.** A deeply discharged lithium pack deteriorates. Top up every few
  months; check the manual for the recommended interval.

## Check the manual

The temperature range differs by model and the manual is the authority. Our
[NOCO GB40 review](/reviews/noco-boost-gb40-jump-starter/) covers one popular unit, and
[the jump starter guide](/guides/best-jump-starters/) explains why peak amps are a
misleading number.

A jump starter is also a **reason to know your battery's age**. If yours is over four
or five years old, see [when to replace a car battery](/blog/car-battery-when-to-replace/)
before you rely on a rescue device for a failing one.

## Bottom line

Don't cook it, don't freeze it, and don't forget about it. **Shade in summer, indoors in
extremes, topped up all year.**
