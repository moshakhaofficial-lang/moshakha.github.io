1200 GSM twisted loop is the spec most serious drying towels land on, and it's worth
understanding why that combination in particular.

## The weave is the important half

**GSM tells you density. The weave tells you behaviour.** Of the two, weave matters more
here, and it's the half that gets less attention.

Twisted loop means the fibres are twisted together into loops rather than standing as
loose plush pile. Those loops create channels that draw water **up into the body of the
towel**, away from the surface in contact with your paint.

The practical difference: a twisted loop towel keeps gliding. A plush towel of the same
weight holds water nearer its surface, and once that surface is saturated it stops
lifting water and starts pushing it around — which is where streaks and water spots come
from.

## Why 1200 specifically

1200 GSM sits at the top of the practical range for a general drying towel:

- Enough capacity to dry a sedan or mid-size SUV without wringing out
- Still light enough to handle when fully loaded
- Deep enough pile to be forgiving on paint

Push higher and you gain capacity while losing manoeuvrability and air-drying speed.
Drop lower and you'll be wringing on anything larger than a small car.

## When it's the wrong choice

**On a small hatchback.** You'll never use the capacity, and you'll fight the weight
around mirrors, badges and door handles. Around 900 GSM in a smaller panel is more
pleasant and just as effective.

**On glass.** Always. Deep pile can't make even contact with a smooth surface, so it
smears the final film of moisture. Glass wants 300–400 GSM — this is true of every
drying towel and is not a fault of this one.

**If you have nowhere to hang it.** A 1200 GSM towel can take a full day to air-dry
properly. Stored damp, microfibre develops mildew that permanently affects absorbency.

## Two mid-weight towels vs one heavy one

For home use, two 900 GSM towels often beat one 1200 GSM towel. You can swap to a
completely dry towel partway through, they're easier to wring, and they launder and dry
far faster.

The single heavy towel wins if you're drying a genuinely large vehicle and want to do it
in one pass without stopping.

## What actually determines performance

In order:

1. **Weave** — twisted loop for drying, always
2. **Blend ratio** — 70/30 beats 80/20 at the same GSM
3. **Edge construction** — suede or edgeless, never a hard stitched border
4. **GSM** — matched to your vehicle, not maximised
